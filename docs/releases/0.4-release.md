# AAC Studio v0.4.0 release

A WILLIAM MCADA PRODUCT

Blueprint 4.8.0 · Exchange contract aac.contract.v4

## Repair scope

Implements the consolidated A01–A16 repair handoff. All 24 current grade/form combinations have been reallocated against repaired contracts, while the original year’s finalized records and historical blueprints remain unchanged.

- Keeps the displayed-data claim and now requires its display.
- Removes prescribed scaling methods, merges the identified conversion and inverse-affine core collisions, and lowers the disputed routine tasks to DOK 1 before reallocation.
- Uses task-based provisional difficulty with an explicit review rationale. Retires unsupported variation-label quotas in favor of intrinsic descriptive categories; mathematical exclusion constraints remain binding.
- Binds the response product and domain rules to reviewed task evidence. Changes invalidate the review even with a fresh hash.
- Adds explicit positive/nonzero, exact-count, feasibility, and estimation-uniqueness conditions. Distinguishes integer piece counts from fractional amounts.
- Supports documented factor-category and cubic-unit aliases, safe SVG quote/whitespace variants, and required exponent/grouped-expression answer shapes.
- Assigns frequency tables and dot plots explicitly rather than letting their encoding determine their meaning.
- Removes solved internal examples and duplicated evidence from generation packets while retaining all 30 adjacent and all paired records.
- Displays v0.4.0 consistently, retains year management and backup/history, and restores v0.3 packets without rewriting their content. Stale drafts require a new packet before importing new work.

The in-app repair register contains an entry for every A01–A16 finding and identifies repairs that still require educator review. It also preserves the earlier audit history.

## Important boundaries

The importer does not infer every numerical parameter from arbitrary prose or prove mathematical equivalence, DOK, workload, or SVG/raster meaning. Domain requirements are now explicit binding contract data, but actual instantiation still needs mathematical review. A field-consistency pass is not a guarantee of correct AI-generated content. Educator review remains mandatory before finalization.

The bank compiler preserves the 4–6 DOK 1 / 8–10 DOK 2 quota, but does not claim psychometric calibration. Difficulty’s 3/9/3 distribution remains a nonblocking target. Retiring inherited variation-label quotas is a deliberate A11 repair, not removal of adjacency restrictions.

No student assessment has been accepted or finalized by this release work. QA examples are test data only.

## Using the offline release

1. Save a JSON backup from your current copy before switching.
2. Open AAC_Studio_v0.4.html in Chrome or Edge. Confirm v0.4.0 and Blueprint 4.8.0 in the header.
3. Restore your backup if using a separate browser/file workspace. Existing finalized forms remain protected. Old drafts are preserved but must receive fresh packets under the repaired bank before new imports.
4. Create a new test year to audit all G5–G7 V1–V8 forms without touching the original assessment year.

Save HTML copy carries the current workspace into a portable file; JSON backups transfer records between copies. Browser history remains local. Offline and hosted editions do not synchronize automatically.
