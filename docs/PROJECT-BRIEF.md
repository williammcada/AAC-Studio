Preserve Dashboard → Generate → Review & Import → Finalize & Export; current and historical bank provenance; packet/design identities; audit views; backup/history; separate years; safe year deletion; optimistic concurrency and failed-save preservation; immutable finalized content; figures; native Word equations; five-part Word package; PDF/browser printing; single-file offline use and product credit.
